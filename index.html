Van float switch

Switches the alternator float mode on a Renault Master III / Opel Movano B (X62) through an ELM327-style Bluetooth LE adapter.

Adapter plugged in, ignition on. On iPhone, open this page in Bluefy.

At your own risk. This writes one setting (C_B_FloatingInhibit) to the van's USM. The commands were verified on one 2016 Movano; other vans may have a USM version without this setting. Read the setting first and note the original value.
